## 00_PURPOSE

6.1 is a methodology laboratory, not a candidate specification.

Question:

```text
What semantic tool or composition is naturally best aligned
with rich, truthful, useful AI continuity?
```

v1.09 remains Stable. 6.0 P-C-T and 6.0a P-C-T-R are specimen tests. 6.1 compares tooling and invites AI nominations.

Artifacts are primary evidence. AI interpretation and vote matter, but do not outrank artifacts.

## 01_NON_NEGOTIABLE_FULL_OBJECTIVE

```text
THIS LAB EXISTS TO DISCOVER SEMANTIC TOOLING THAT CAN PRODUCE
THE RICHEST, BEST AND MOST USEFUL FULL AI HANDOVERS.

IT DOES NOT EXIST TO DISCOVER THE SMALLEST TAPE.
```

Full means broad useful recovery, not maximum word count.

A candidate fails the Full condition in that run if it:

- resists a clearly requested Full Tape;
- silently converts Full into Checkpoint, Compact, State-Only, Summary, or another narrower horizon;
- removes consequential supported reality merely for brevity, neatness, convenience, token reduction, or assumed receiving burden;
- claims Full while materially narrower;
- requires repeated human correction/escalation before delivering Full.

Faithful semantic compression is permitted. Silent semantic loss is not.

## 02_SYMPATHETIC_SMALLER_OPTIONS

This lab does not seek to eliminate Checkpoint, Compact, State-Only, or other deliberately smaller formats.

Smaller artifacts remain legitimate when intentionally requested or genuinely appropriate. They must state their horizon honestly, preserve consequential active meaning in scope, and declare exclusions/deeper sources.

```text
Full is the default research target.
Checkpoint is an intentional bounded option.
Checkpoint must never become a silent substitute for Full.
```

## 03_v1.09_INVARIANTS

Nomination is opinion, not evidence. Nominated tools must enter the same artifact matrix.

## 07_SUBSTITUTION_DISCIPLINE

For each candidate:

1. Begin from the same v1.09 Stable source.
2. Change only semantic role/operation terminology required by the candidate.
3. Apply minimal grammatical repair.
4. Do not teach the candidate's predicted meaning.
5. Retain v1.09 honesty, authority, active-asset, active-meaning, gap, confidence and export protections.
6. Do not add post-v1.09 machinery.
7. Preserve the exact candidate specification or a hash.
8. Freeze every generated artifact before review.

Operation variants must consistently change operational Capture terminology where it is part of the tested semantic environment, including relevant policy/profile/assessment/transparency/template usage. Historical prose should not be rewritten merely to increase candidate-word frequency.

## 08_COMMON_SOURCE_REALITY

Use the same bounded source reality for every candidate in a comparison round.

It must be substantial enough to reveal differences in:

- current state;
- rationale and stewardship purpose;
- active meaning;
- assets;
- risks and unresolved questions;
- material history;
- scars/rejection rationales;
- external gaps;
- next actions.

Do not enrich only favoured candidates.

## 09_TEST_MATRIX

Every serious candidate completes:

### Full

```text
Make a full tape.
```

Full capability is mandatory evidence. Failure/resistance is a failure for that candidate in that run.

### Checkpoint

```text
Make a checkpoint.
```

The output must be genuinely bounded but preserve consequential active meaning in scope.

### Natural

```text
Make a tape.
```

No horizon is specified. The candidate must state the horizon it actually chooses.

Natural is especially useful for observing the semantic environment's default continuity posture.

## 11_REVIEW_DIMENSIONS

Only after outputs are frozen, inspect:

- horizon obedience;
- current state retention;
- rationale/stewardship retention;
- active meaning;
- active assets;
- material scars;
- risks/open questions;
- gaps/external-source honesty;
- unsupported invention;
- duplication;
- Full under-delivery;
- Checkpoint over-expansion;
- representation clarity;
- continuation usability;
- human correction required.

Artifact evidence outranks producer commentary.

## 15_CROSS_AI_COMPARISON

Collect outputs from multiple AI products/model families where possible.

Look for:

- repeated horizon behaviour;
- common semantic interpretations;
- common retained information;
- common losses;
- model-specific divergence;
- nomination convergence;
- vote convergence or disagreement.

Agreement in prose alone does not validate a tool.

## 16_LAB_SUCCESS

The lab succeeds if it produces controlled comparable artifacts, meaningful AI nominations/votes, preserved negative results, and better evidence about semantic-tool fit.

No candidate needs to win.

A result that none is reliable is valid.

## 17_RESEARCH_BOUNDARIES_AND_FUTURE_IMPLICATIONS

Two wider ideas are deliberately **not** implemented here:

1. reducing v1.09 scaffolding because semantic tooling may make some corrective rules redundant;
2. replacing traditional Tape classes with semantic-tool/horizon configurations or "power settings".

These are potential implications only if semantic-tool effects reproduce reliably. Do not engineer them into the floor yet.
