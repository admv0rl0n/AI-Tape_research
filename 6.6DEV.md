6.5 survives truncation through prefix-valid design and budgeted profiles — but often loses decision-surface quality and agency.

Goal of 6.6:
Deliver the strongest possible successor restoration that still remains useful even when truncated.Useful restoration remains the primary objective.
Truncation resistance is a hard constraint.
Smallness is not a virtue.01_DESIGN_POSTUREKeep the floor low and honest.
Prefix-valid is mandatory.
Force density is the primary success metric.
Agency must be preserved.
Epistemic hygiene is non-negotiable.
Compression and profiles are tools, not goals.
Graceful degradation: lose secondary detail first, protect decision surface as long as possible.
Optional richness must never raise the minimum landing requirement.
Meaning survives. Weight does not.
