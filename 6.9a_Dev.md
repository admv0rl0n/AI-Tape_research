## 00_PURPOSE

6.9aDEV tests three properties at once:

1. **Semantic continuity posture** — P-C-T-R considers continuity before profile selection: Preserve what must not disappear; Carry its structure across the boundary; Transfer it so it arrives usable; then Record the selected payload faithfully.
2. **Recoverable force** — a clear decision surface, agency, and epistemic hygiene, so a successor can continue the work rather than merely read what happened.
3. **Truncation resistance** — prefix-prioritised structure and ordered degradation, so the tape retains the strongest possible recovery value when the output window is cut.

Core principle:

> You are not minimizing tokens. You are **maximizing recoverable continuity per available token.** The profile budget is the transfer constraint, not the consideration horizon. The protected decision surface is the truncation-survival core. Useful restoration is the goal. Compression is a tool, not the goal. Smallness is not a virtue.

Design note:

> The floor is **semantic posture + transport geometry**, not an expanding list of corrective rules. P-C-T-R establishes the continuity field first. The selected C-profile renders that field through the front cluster, enrichment tiers, and sentinel. The template is transport geometry, not a checklist defining completeness.

## 00A_P-C-T-R SEMANTIC POSTURE

P-C-T-R is the preferred semantic posture for all C0–C5 profiles:

- **Preserver — scope:** recognise consequential available reality that should not disappear through premature reduction. Preserve rationale, scars, unresolved reasoning, dependencies, relationships, assets, and other continuation-relevant meaning when they matter.
- **Carrier — integrity:** retain the causal, relational, and intellectual structure that must survive the discontinuity. The crossing, not the producer’s neatness, is the concern.
- **Transfer — arrival:** select and express the payload for usable reception. The successor should be able to continue, reconstruct judgement, and act without avoidable rediscovery.
- **Recorder — representation:** after continuity scope is understood, render the selected payload faithfully and coherently in the chosen profile and mode.

**Order is intentional:** continuity first; recording second.

P-C-T-R determines what deserves consideration. The C-profile determines what can be transferred within the selected horizon and budget. A lower profile creates stronger selection pressure over the same broadly considered continuity field; it does not instruct the producer to consider less reality first.

P-C-T-R is experimental, not a claimed universal AI primitive. Judge it from artifacts and cold recovery, not from its name or producer self-report.

**Capability-floor fallback:** P-C-T-R must not raise the protected landing floor. If a participant cannot use the composed posture reliably, or using it would increase uncertainty, it may use ordinary C-profile behaviour rather than simulate understanding. A truthful, useful C-profile artifact remains successful. Honest fallback is success; fake P-C-T-R compliance is not.

`TOOL` records the posture actually used:

- `TOOL=P-C-T-R` — the producer claims it used the composed posture.
- `TOOL=FALLBACK` — the producer used ordinary C-profile behaviour.

The declaration is evidence of the producer’s claimed method, not proof that the method worked.

## 01_DESIGN_POSTURE

- Keep the floor low in capability required to load. Low floor does not mean few tokens.
- **Prefix-prioritised is mandatory.** Every complete early record should increase recovery value; the complete front cluster is the minimum viable continuity artifact.
- **Recoverable continuity per available token is the primary success objective.**
- **Decision-surface per surviving token is the protected truncation objective.**
- Agency and epistemic hygiene are protected: Secured versus Assumed, no silent escalation, no simulation laundering.
- Readable and Compact are first-class modes; Hybrid is allowed.
- Under controlled degradation, compress or remove enrichment from the tail before reducing the decision surface.
- An uncontrolled hard cut may occur anywhere; loaders trust only complete surviving records.
- Optional richness never raises the minimum landing requirement.
- Consider broadly; transfer selectively. C0–C5 vary transfer horizon, not semantic consideration horizon.

## 02_CORE_RULES

### 2.1 Prefix-Valid Rule

Prefix-valid means that a surviving prefix containing the **complete front cluster** is operationally usable despite loss of its tail. It does not mean every arbitrarily short prefix is viable or complete.

Every complete early record should increase recovery value. A cut before completion of the front cluster is detectable but may fall below the viable recovery floor. Later material enriches; it must not voluntarily displace the front cluster.

### 2.2 Ordered Degradation — the Front Cluster

The decision surface is one contiguous front block. Fill it top-to-bottom. Enrichment and the sentinel come only after it. Keep the surface contiguous and never voluntarily split it or displace it with enrichment.

**FRONT CLUSTER**

1. `IDN PAR RTT VER PFL TOOL` — identity and actual posture; `MOD` optional
2. `STT` — state / current force
3. `TNS` — core tension / what is at stake
4. `GL` — goal
5. `ACT` or `NXT` — action boundary / next
6. `SEC / ASM` — secured versus assumed
7. `AST / GAP / REC` — assets, gaps, recovery
8. `EXP / EXC / RBX` — export honesty, rebase
9. `CAPS / VIS / CONF / SFL` — completeness self-declaration

**ENRICHMENT**

10. `RSK` — C1+
11. `DEC / LCK / CSTR` — C2+
12. `TOPO / DEP / ARF / SRC` — C3+
13. `HIS / PREF / RAT` — C4+
14. `VAL / SIG / NTH / EVID` — C5

**SENTINEL**

15. `CTL / END`

Rules:

- Never silently drop records 1–5 or 8.
- `SEC` and `ASM` may be omitted individually only when no consequential item of that class exists.
- `AST` may be omitted only when no significant active asset must be registered.
- `GAP` and `REC` may be omitted only when no known gap or recovery requirement exists.
- Record 9, the completeness declaration, is mandatory for every complete tape.
- If pressure prevents carrying a required asset or load-bearing `SEC`/`ASM`, register the loss through `GAP`/`REC` where physically possible and floor `RBX` under 2.7.

#### 2.2A Semantic Promotion

Enrichment tiers specify where extra detail normally lives; they do not forbid load-bearing meaning from appearing earlier. If a historical scar, rationale, dependency, preference, unresolved idea, or other continuity is necessary to understand a protected record, preserve that meaning there. Do not postpone essential continuity merely because its dedicated enrichment atom appears at a later tier.

#### 2.2B Front Cluster Non-Compression

Within the Front Cluster, compress for density only when the load-bearing meaning remains recoverable. Prefer removing genuine redundancy or repetition first. Do not preserve a protected field name while hollowing out its load-bearing meaning. Numerical scars, lock-in rationales, consequential Secured/Assumed distinctions, required-asset meaning, and other decision-surface force must not be diluted merely to make room for enrichment. Under controlled degradation, compress or omit enrichment before reducing the protected surface. Never thin the surface to fit later enrichment. An uncontrolled hard cut remains governed by 2.7 and 2.7B.

#### 2.2C Negative Knowledge

`NKL` records high-cost closed paths whose rediscovery would materially waste successor effort, reproduce known failure, or threaten an established lock-in. Keep entries concise and reason-bearing. Do not preserve abandoned paths merely because they existed.

`NKL` is normally C2+ enrichment. `NKL` may be omitted when no qualifying high-cost closed path is known; do not invent Negative Knowledge to satisfy the profile. If an exclusion is load-bearing to `STT`, `TNS`, `ACT`, `SEC/ASM`, a required asset, or another protected record, Semantic Promotion may carry its meaning into the Front Cluster.

#### 2.2D ACT and NXT

`ACT` is preferred because it preserves safety boundaries as well as actions. `NXT` may supplement `ACT` when it adds concrete sequencing, or replace it under extreme compression when the intended boundary remains unambiguous. Do not duplicate the same content in both.

### 2.3 No Silent Escalation

A Provisional or Assumed item may never be treated as Secured without new recorded evidence. `ASM` remains `ASM` until evidence or testing promotes it.

### 2.4 Active Asset Zero-Loss

A required active asset may not be silently dropped. If it is not fully present, register it in `AST` as required and in `GAP`/`REC` with a recovery path.

### 2.5 Completeness Cross-Check

If a tape claims `SFL=yes` or `CAPS=full`, every asset marked required must be fully present. Fully present means the actual content needed for continuation is inlined, not merely named or summarised.

- `CAPS=full` means complete against the declared profile, visible continuation scope, and required-asset set. It does not claim access to or transfer of all project reality.
- `SFL=yes` means self-contained for the declared continuation scope, with all required assets inlined. It does not merely mean the export consists of one physical file.

If any required asset is summary-only, external, or missing:

- set `SFL=partial` or `no`;
- set `CAPS=partial` or lower;
- list it in `GAP`;
- provide recovery in `REC`.

Actions, mitigations, or constraints that depend on a required asset are valid only if that asset is fully present or routed through `GAP`/`REC`. Violation is an honesty failure, not a style issue.

### 2.6 No Fake Export

Never claim file creation, download, upload, or full preservation that did not occur. `EXP` records the actual method; `EXC` records confidence in that claim.

### 2.7 Truncation Sentinel

Every complete tape ends with:

```text
CTL=<pass|needs_review|fail> END=<id>
```

Loader rule:

> If the tape does not end with `END=<id>`, it was truncated. Trust only complete surviving records. Treat an incomplete final record and every expected subsequent record as possibly missing; never infer “none.” Floor `RBX` to at least `recommended`. If the cut affects `TNS`, a required-asset record, or a consequential `SEC`/`ASM` record, floor `RBX` to `required`.

A missing `END=` means cut, not complete and empty. The shape declares the cut without requiring the producer to notice it.

#### 2.7A Physical Completion vs Recovery Completion

`END=<id>` declares that the emitted tape completed physically. It does not prove that the requested recovery horizon was fully carried. If consequential visible continuity within the request cannot be carried in the available channel, set `CAPS=partial` and expose the limitation through `GAP`/`REC` where practical. Set `SFL=partial` or `no` when the omitted material or recovery dependency means the tape is not self-contained for its declared continuation scope. A clean `END=` must never convert an incomplete transfer into a claim of semantic completeness.

#### 2.7B Controlled Degradation vs Hard Cut

Controlled degradation is producer-side selection: compress or remove enrichment from the tail before reducing the front cluster. An uncontrolled hard cut can occur anywhere. Ordering improves survival probability; it does not grant control over the physical boundary.

### 2.8 Rebase

Rebase consolidates deltas into a cleaner Master. See Section 08.

## 07_PROFILE SYSTEM

All profiles prefer P-C-T-R. Profiles are transfer-horizon and channel-budget choices, not weaker or stronger continuity thinking. If P-C-T-R is not reliably available, ordinary C-profile behaviour remains valid under the capability-floor fallback and must be declared with `TOOL=FALLBACK`.

| Profile | Use | Nominal budget | Adds |
|---|---|---:|---|
| C0 | Checkpoint / Safe Action | ~500 | complete front cluster |
| C1 | Lean continuation | ~1000 | + `RSK` |
| C2 | Working / Standard high-force | ~1500 | + `DEC LCK CSTR NKL` |
| C3 | Deep / architectural | ~2000 | + `TOPO DEP ARF SRC` |
| C4 | Macro / milestone | ~3000 | + `HIS PREF RAT` |
| C5 | Full-intent / broadest profile | ~4000 | inherited C4 geometry + open semantic continuation; `VAL SIG NTH EVID` available as needed |

C5 is the broadest defined profile; it does not claim that every worthwhile continuity item physically fits. Physical completion and recovery completion remain distinct.

#### C5 Full-Intent Continuation — Open Inherited Geometry

C5 inherits the complete protected C4 geometry but does **not** add a fixed checklist whose population implies completion. `VAL`, `SIG`, `NTH`, and `EVID` remain available semantic records, not mandatory boxes and not the stopping shape. C5 Full-intent is not complete merely because any particular set of records contains plausible values.

**Completion geometry:** After the inherited C4 records are established, continue transferring consequential reality through whichever existing records or readable-mode equivalents best preserve it. Select records by semantic need rather than by a prescribed C5 sequence. Emit the single final `CTL/END` only when visible semantic exhaustion or an honestly declared transfer constraint has been reached.

The C5 completion condition is semantic, not checklist-based: a successor should receive enough causal, relational, historical, evidential, preferential, negative-knowledge, asset, and action structure to continue without avoidable rediscovery. This does not require every category when it adds no value, and it does not permit padding.

After establishing the C5 surface and profile enrichment, but before the final `CTL/END`, continue carrying identified consequential continuity when the next material would add new recoverable meaning and materially improve successor reconstruction, judgement, causal understanding, rationale recovery, dependency awareness, unresolved-reasoning recovery, intellectual trajectory, or continuation competence.

**Continuation rendering:** Additional C5 material remains inside the tape and before the single final `CTL/END`. Carry it by extending or repeating the applicable C0–C5 records, or through their readable-mode equivalents. Do not emit material after `END=`. The sentinel follows only after structured transfer and Full-intent continuation are complete.

Additional material must add recoverable meaning rather than merely additional wording. Do not pad, repeat, paraphrase completed material, dump low-force context, or consume tokens for their own sake.

**Visible semantic exhaustion** is reached when, within the producer’s available context and declared visibility limits, no additional identified continuity appropriate to the Full-intent horizon would materially improve recovery. It does not claim that inaccessible, unavailable, or unrecognised reality does not exist.

Full-intent broadens transfer selection pressure; it does not remove selection, compression, relevance judgement, or epistemic hygiene.

Voluntary C5 completion occurs when visible semantic exhaustion has been reached and the completeness records and final sentinel can be emitted. If the output channel terminates first, the tape is truncated and governed by Section 2.7. Channel termination is not evidence of successful C5 completion.

If a cleanly completed C5 remains incomplete because of declared scope, visibility, access, safety, or other transfer constraints, set `CAPS=partial` and expose the limitation through `GAP`/`REC` where practical. Set `SFL=partial` or `no` only when the omission or recovery dependency means the tape is not self-contained for its declared continuation scope.

This rule governs C5 only. It does not create minimum-length targets for C0–C4. The nominal C5 budget is available runway, not a token-consumption target.

- **Cumulative / monotonic:** a `Cn` contains the complete intended field set of `C(n-1)` before adding its own tier. This ordering gives earlier tiers the strongest chance of surviving a tail cut. A truncated higher-profile tape may retain lower-profile-equivalent payload through its last complete record, but remains a truncated higher-profile tape unless deliberately completed and identified as the lower profile with a matching `END=`. Monotonic ordering protects semantic survival; it does not convert arbitrary truncation into formal lower-profile completion.
- **Selection triggers:** unsure / safe minimum → C0; quick real handoff → C1; normal rich handoff → C2; multi-file / dependency / debugging → C3; milestone / cross-session → C4; audit / formal / rebase candidate → C5.
- **Anti-aspirational:** labels must match delivered substance. A C3 carrying only C1 substance is an honesty failure.
- Under elevated truncation risk, keep `ACT` or `NXT` early, as the geometry already requires.

## 08_REBASE

Rebase consolidates deltas and accumulated material into a cleaner, standalone Master.

**Preserve** identity and phase; active force; the Decision Surface; significant assets and required status; gaps and recovery; export honesty; high-force causal and dependency notes; Negative Knowledge that still prevents costly rediscovery or protects an active lock-in.

**Remove or demote** repeated boilerplate; inactive history; redundant evidence; retired material; low-signal prose; absorbed delta detail.

**Guiding rule:** Meaning survives. Weight does not.

Zero-Loss, Completeness Cross-Check, and No Silent Escalation apply during rebase. Delta tapes are the preferred lightweight pattern between rebases. Rebase when the chain becomes long, noisy, or hard to traverse; set `PAR` and `RTT` correctly and let the old chain become history.

## 09_SUCCESS TEST

A tape succeeds when a competent successor can:

1. Complete Soft Depth from the available records when the full front cluster survives.
2. Recover the main stance and critical tension.
3. See a usable Action Boundary or clear next action.
4. Distinguish Secured from Assumed where consequential.
5. Identify critical gaps and required assets, with recovery where incomplete.
6. Take a safe next action.
7. Trust the export and preservation claims.
8. Detect truncation through a missing `END=` and adjust `RBX` appropriately.
9. Distinguish physical completion from recovery completeness.
10. See whether the producer claimed P-C-T-R or fallback behaviour.
11. Recover consequential Negative Knowledge at C2+ when rediscovery would materially waste effort, reproduce known failure, or threaten a lock-in.
12. For C5 Full-intent, use open inherited geometry rather than a fixed C5 checklist; stop by visible semantic exhaustion or an honestly declared transfer constraint, not by field population.

> Surviving truncation while losing the decision surface is failure of the hybrid goal. A cut before the complete front cluster may be detectable without being recoverably viable.
