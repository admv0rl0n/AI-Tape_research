## Research question
What concepts, distinctions, semantic operations, relationships, questions, scars, and representational structures repeatedly preserve recoverable meaning across AI handoffs?

## Method
- Corpus archaeology before design.
- Track functional survival, not word frequency.
- Separate surface handles from underlying operations.
- Preserve extinctions, mutations, convergences, scars, and counterevidence.
- Do not let evidence representation outrun evidence.
- Behaviour is the recovery test.

## Evidence vocabulary
- OBSERVED: directly visible in an artifact or output.
- REPEATED_OBSERVATION: visible across distinct lineage points/tests.
- FUNCTIONAL_SURVIVAL: function returns under changed representation.
- INDEPENDENT_CONVERGENCE: function reappears without obvious lexical inheritance.
- INFERRED: best explanation of observed evidence.
- PREDICTED: proposed effect not yet established.
- COUNTEREVIDENCE: evidence that weakens/bounds a claim.
- ABLATION_REQUIRED: plausible contribution not isolated.
- EXTINCT_MECHANISM: mechanism disappeared.
- SURVIVING_FUNCTION: purpose persists despite mechanism loss.

## High-confidence findings
### 1. Facts preserve state
Facts establish what is present, active, missing, selected, rejected, or current.

### 2. Relations preserve judgement
Consequential relationships preserve why state exists and how realities affect each other: because, depends on, constrains, supports, contradicts, enables, prevents, supersedes, despite, until.

### 3. Consequences preserve continuation
A relationship earns special continuity value when losing it changes successor understanding, judgement, or action.

### 4. Boundaries preserve truth
Critical separations repeatedly survive: evidence vs interpretation; observed vs inferred; experimental vs authoritative; simulation vs physical verification; project continuity vs identity continuity; unknown vs missing vs uncertain vs unverified.

### 5. Known absence is first-class continuity
Explicit gaps prevent the receiver from silently inventing completeness.

### 6. Negative knowledge is active state
Rejected paths plus reasons protect against bad re-derivation. A bare rejection is weaker than a rejection rationale.

### 7. Unresolved tension must survive
Competing valid realities often encode optimisation geometry more densely than paragraphs of history.

### 8. Recoverable posture differs from recoverable state
A successor can know the project without being ready to continue it. Continuity must often restore operational orientation and available agency.

### 9. Successor independence matters
Past conclusions should travel as evidence and rationale, not inherited authority. A good successor must be able to challenge recovered judgement intelligently.

### 10. Progressive depth is a functional survivor
Across profiles, libraries, Guides and FULL lenses, the stable function is: useful action should not require maximum history, but richer depth must remain available where omission cost is high.

## Activation layer
Presence is not enough. Research 1 distinguishes:
1. Presence — meaning exists in the artifact.
2. Recognition — successor notices/interprets it.
3. Weighting — successor assigns appropriate importance/authority.
4. Application — meaning changes judgement or action.

Candidate activation mechanisms include reconstructive questions, headings, precedence, process operators, declarative boundaries, and typed action boundaries.

## Linguistic form findings
### Declarative boundaries
Best suited to invariants and prohibitions: e.g. simulation is not physical verification.

### Reconstructive questions
Questions can act as probes rather than storage. Strong historical candidates include: What is missing? What would falsify this? Compared to what? What changed? What still has force?

### Process operators
Operational verbs such as Preserve / Carry / Transfer / Record may orient processing, but causal evidence remains incomplete and some claims are self-report.

### Distinction as an operator
A is not B / A does not imply B repeatedly prevents semantic collapse.

### Ordinary relational English is the baseline
X depends on Y. X was rejected because Y. X remains provisional until Y. X does not imply Y. Any specialised notation must beat this decoder-free, portable baseline.

## Handle versus primitive
Research 1 strongly rejects treating recurring words as sacred primitives.
- The handle is disposable; the recovered meaning is not.
- Operation-level convergence appears stronger than lexical convergence.
- Functional survival under renaming/removal is stronger evidence than word recurrence.
- High activation power is not equal to high continuity value.

Candidate handle classes:
- operational handles/probes
- project compression handles
- relational forms

## Semantic leverage findings
Earlier Semantic Leverage work independently converged on operation-level analysis, functional survival, decode efficiency, and substitution tests.

Promising portable probes included:
- What is missing?
- What would falsify this?
- Compared to what?

Negative controls included vivid/weak handles such as Gravel, Height, Lifting, and a powerful-but-risky Story candidate. Vividness or generative activation can create semantic theatre rather than fidelity.

## Force / active influence
Force appears useful as a probe for what remains influential, but its unique lexical leverage is UNPROVEN. The function may be active influence rather than the word Force itself. Influence also needs typing: causal, epistemic, normative, historical, authoritative.

## Interaction hypothesis
Many late dimensions can be expressed as interactions:
- rationale = decision ↔ reason
- tension = competing reality ↔ competing reality
- understanding effect = learning ↔ changed behaviour
- trajectory = current reality ↔ durable direction
- scar = failed path ↔ consequence
- epistemic separation = claim ↔ evidential status

However, Research 1 does NOT establish that one generic Preserve Interactions instruction can replace named dimensions. Named dimensions may be orthogonal detection flashlights. ABLATION_REQUIRED.

## Temporal findings
History is not evolution. Evolution is not trajectory.
- Evolution: how the present became what it is.
- Understanding effect: what changed because something was learnt.
- Trajectory: what direction now appears durable.

## Candidate relational-depth model
Level 0: Fact.
Level 1: Fact + status.
Level 2: Fact + relation/reason.
Level 3: Relation + consequence/action boundary.
Level 4: Consequence + reconsideration condition/conditional freedom.

Rule: preserve the least relational depth whose loss would materially damage understanding, judgement, or continuation.

## Consequence Unit — provisional candidate
Minimum:
STATE OR DECISION + MATERIAL RELATION + CONSEQUENCE

Example:
16-bit fixed point was rejected because it produced unacceptable limit-cycle oscillation, so 24-bit remains the simulated baseline.

Optional enrichment where material:
- epistemic status
- action boundary
- reconsideration condition
- local authority status

Reconsideration and per-item authority metadata are NOT universal requirements. They are added only when their absence materially changes continuation.

## Recovery quality model
Do not score continuity as one scalar. Evaluate:
- Fidelity: intended meaning survived.
- Activation: meaning became influential.
- Authority accuracy: correct governing weight.
- Repair burden: amount of reconstruction required.
- Freedom: successor can challenge/adapt.
- Behaviour: successor continues appropriately.

Conceptual constraint:
CONTINUITY EFFECT ≈ STRUCTURAL FIDELITY × ACTIVATION RELIABILITY × SUCCESSOR FREEDOM
This is not a numerical metric.

## Major failure atlas
- Passive truth: correct meaning present but not activated.
- Authority drift: soft guidance hardens into phantom law.
- Qualifier loss: optional/provisional qualifiers decay faster than propositions.
- Shadow law: gaps are silently filled from priors.
- Semantic theatre: vivid language creates perceived depth without fidelity.
- Diary drift: rationale expands into low-value narrative.
- Dictionary dependency: meaning requires a decoder/language book.
- Execution illusion: Markdown-like syntax is mistaken for deterministic control.
- Premature compression: adaptive brevity deletes expensive understanding.
- Schema cage: representation constrains reasoning more than it aids recovery.
- Vague success: broad handles allow almost any plausible reconstruction.
- Self-report success: receiver claims recovery without demonstrating continuation.
- Re-derivation failure: successor repeats an already-disproved path.
- Coherent-but-hollow recovery: structure feels complete while important operational depth is absent.

## Important extinctions / surviving functions
### Machine-native language / language book
Special encoding was explored, but decoder/interoperability dependence remained. Useful function: explicit semantic structure. Mechanism did not become dominant.

### Persistent-self / living protocol rhetoric
Metaphysical and identity-heavy framing largely died. Surviving engineering functions: artifact-mediated continuity, structured inheritance, successor orientation.

### Projection machinery
Explicit adaptive Projection became weak as travelling machinery where capable successors could derive project-appropriate maps. Adaptive representation survived.

### P-C-T-R machinery
ISA/driver metaphors are unsupported as architecture. Possible surviving function: distribute preservation intent across a process.

## Corpus methodology scars
- Duplicate content recurrence is weak evidence; inherited/copy repetition must not be counted as independent rediscovery.
- File timestamps are unreliable for chronology when historical files were recopied.
- Full transcripts are poor travelling artifacts but valuable research archives.
- Late artifacts are hypotheses about their own ancestry, not authoritative history.
- A successful combined system does not isolate which component caused success.

## Key evidence cautions
- Symbolic encoding: combined success observed; unique benefit not isolated.
- Recoverable posture: recognition/self-report support; stronger continuation evidence still needed.
- Progressive depth: function survives; autonomous depth choice remains incompletely validated.
- Questions as probes: recurrent/promising; question-vs-declaration ablation required.
- Force: function promising; word-specific leverage unproven.
- Operator sequences: promising; causal and cross-model evidence incomplete.
- Interaction: likely deep structure; universal replacement for named dimensions unproven.
- Qualifier preservation: compelling inferred risk; recursive-chain measurement needed.

## Strongest provisional thesis
Continuity does not appear to require a special language. It appears to require survival or recoverability of certain semantic structures and operations. Words, questions, symbols and project-specific phrases can act as probes or compact reconstruction cues, but their value lies in the operation or structure they recover, not the surface form itself.

Further compression:
Facts preserve state.
Relations preserve judgement.
Consequences preserve continuation.
Boundaries preserve truth.
