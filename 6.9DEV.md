## 00_PURPOSE

6.9 delivers three properties:

1. **Semantic continuity posture (P-C-T-R)** — Preserve what must not disappear; Carry what must survive the boundary; Transfer it so it arrives usable; then Record the established payload faithfully.
2. **Recoverable force** — clear decision surface, agency, and epistemic hygiene so a successor can continue the work.
3. **Truncation resistance** — prefix-valid structure and ordered degradation so the tape remains useful when hard-cut.

Core principle:

> You are not minimizing tokens. You are **maximizing recoverable continuity per available token.**  
> The profile budget is the transfer constraint, not the consideration horizon.  
> The protected decision surface is the truncation-survival core.  
> Useful restoration is the goal. Compression is a tool, not the goal.  
> Smallness is not a virtue.

Design note:

> The floor is built as **semantic posture + transport geometry**.  
> P-C-T-R establishes the continuity field first. The selected C-profile renders that field through the front cluster, enrichment tiers, and sentinel.  
> The template is transport geometry, not a checklist that defines completeness.

## 00A_P-C-T-R SEMANTIC POSTURE

P-C-T-R is the preferred semantic posture for all C0–C5 profiles:

- **Preserver** — recognise consequential reality that should not disappear through premature reduction.
- **Carrier** — keep the causal, relational, and intellectual structure that must survive the discontinuity.
- **Transfer** — prefer usable arrival so the receiver can continue without avoidable rediscovery.
- **Recorder** — after continuity scope is understood, represent the selected payload faithfully in the chosen profile and mode.

Order is intentional: continuity first; recording second.

P-C-T-R determines what deserves consideration. The C-profile determines what can be transferred in the current budget. A lower profile applies stronger selection pressure; it does not authorise shallower consideration.

**Capability-floor fallback:** If P-C-T-R cannot be used reliably, fall back to ordinary C-profile behaviour. Honest fallback is success; simulated compliance is not.

## 01_DESIGN_POSTURE

- Keep the floor low in *capability required to load* (a 14B can read it). Low floor does not mean few tokens.
- Prefix-valid is mandatory.
- Recoverable continuity per available token is the primary success objective.
- Decision-surface per surviving token is the protected truncation objective.
- Agency and epistemic hygiene are protected.
- Two first-class modes: Readable and Compact. Hybrid is allowed.
- Graceful degradation: protect the decision surface; cut from the tail.
- Optional richness never raises the minimum landing requirement.
- Consider broadly; transfer selectively.

## 02_CORE_RULES

### 2.1 Prefix-Valid Rule
The beginning of every tape forms a usable minimum continuity artifact. Later material only enriches.

### 2.2 Ordered Degradation — the Front Cluster

FRONT CLUSTER (never split, never pushed to the tail):  
1. IDN PAR RTT VER PFL (MOD optional)  
2. STT  
3. TNS  
4. GL  
5. ACT  
6. SEC / ASM  
6b. NKL (C2+)  
7. AST / GAP / REC  
8. EXP / EXC / RBX  
9. CAPS / VIS / CONF / SFL  

ENRICHMENT (after the surface):  
10. RSK (C1+)  
11. DEC / LCK / CSTR (C2+)  
12. TOPO / DEP / SRC (C3+)  
13. HIS / RAT (C4+)  
14. SIG / NTH / EVID (C5)  

SENTINEL:  
15. CTL / END  

Never silently drop lines 1–5 and 8.  
Lines 6–7 and 9 may be omitted only when genuinely empty. Required assets or load-bearing SEC/ASM that cannot fit go into GAP/REC and floor RBX.

#### 2.2A Semantic Promotion
Load-bearing scars, rationale, or dependencies may appear in the Front Cluster when continuity requires it. Do not postpone essential force merely because its enrichment atom sits later.

#### 2.2B Front Cluster Non-Compression
Inside the Front Cluster, values may be shortened only to remove genuine redundancy or pure repetition. Load-bearing force, numerical scars, lock-in rationales, and Secured/Assumed distinctions must not be diluted or moved to enrichment under budget pressure. If space is insufficient, stop after the last complete Front Cluster line and omit the sentinel. The missing `END=` signals truncation. Never thin the surface to fit later enrichment.

#### 2.2C Negative Knowledge Promotion
For C2 and above, carry the 1–3 highest-cost closed paths in `NKL` inside or immediately after the Front Cluster. Use one-line reasons. Goal: early survival of the exclusions a successor is most likely to re-derive.

### 2.3 No Silent Escalation
Assumed items stay Assumed until new evidence promotes them.

### 2.4 Active Asset Zero-Loss
Required active assets may not be silently dropped. If not fully present they must appear in AST (marked required) and in GAP/REC with recovery path.

### 2.5 Completeness Cross-Check
If SFL=yes or CAPS=full is claimed, every required asset must be fully present (content inlined, not merely named). Otherwise set SFL/CAPS to partial or lower, list in GAP, and provide REC.

### 2.6 No Fake Export
Never claim file creation or preservation that did not occur.

### 2.7 Truncation Sentinel
Every complete tape ends with:  
`CTL=<pass|needs_review|fail> END=<id>`

If `END=<id>` is missing, the tape was truncated. Floor RBX accordingly. Never infer “none” for material after the last complete line.

#### 2.7A Physical vs Recovery Completion
`END=<id>` means the emission completed physically. It does not prove the requested recovery horizon was fully carried. Incomplete Full-intent transfers keep CAPS=partial and SFL=partial.

### 2.8 Rebase
See section 08.

## 07_PROFILE SYSTEM

All profiles prefer P-C-T-R. Profiles are transfer-horizon choices, not permission to consider less reality.

| Profile | Budget | Name | Adds |
|:-------:|-------:|------|------|
| C0 | ~500 | Checkpoint / Safe Action | Full decision surface |
| C1 | ~1000 | Lean continuation | + RSK |
| C2 | ~1500 | Working / Standard | + DEC LCK CSTR |
| C3 | ~2000 | Deep / architectural | + TOPO DEP SRC |
| C4 | ~3000 | Macro / milestone | + HIS RAT |
| C5 | ~4000 | Full-intent | + SIG NTH EVID |

Cumulative and monotonic: cutting a C5 at 2000 tokens yields a valid C3; at 500 tokens yields a valid C0.

**C5 Full-Intent Exhaustion**  
A populated C5 template is not proof of completion. Continue carrying visible consequential continuity while it would materially improve recovery and capacity remains. Stop when useful continuity is exhausted or the channel ends. Short C5 is valid when nothing further would improve recovery. If consequential material remains uncarried, set CAPS=partial and SFL=partial.
