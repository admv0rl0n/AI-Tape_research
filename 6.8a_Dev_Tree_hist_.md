## 00_PURPOSE

6.8 delivers two properties at once:

1. **Recoverable force** — a clear decision surface, agency, and epistemic hygiene,
   so a successor can actually *continue* the work, not just read what happened.
2. **Truncation resistance** — prefix-valid structure and ordered degradation, so the
   tape stays useful even when the output window is hard-cut.

Core principle:

> You are not minimizing tokens. You are **maximizing preserved decision-surface per
> available token.** The budget is the constraint; the decision surface is the
> objective. Useful restoration is the goal. Compression is a tool, not the goal.
> Smallness is not a virtue.

Design note (for maintainers, not loaders):

> The floor is built as **templates to fill, not rules to obey.** Long "must/never"

- Keep the floor **low** — meaning low *capability* to load (a 14B can read it).

## 02_CORE_RULES

### 2.1 Prefix-Valid Rule
The beginning of every tape forms a usable minimum continuity artifact. Later material
only enriches. A cut from the tail must never remove the front cluster before it
removes the enrichment and the sentinel.

### 2.2 Ordered Degradation — the Front Cluster
The **decision surface is one front block.** Fill top-to-bottom; enrichment and the
sentinel come only *after* the surface.

FRONT CLUSTER (the decision surface — never split, never pushed to the tail): 1 IDN PAR RTT VER PFL (identity; MOD optional) 2 STT (state / current force) 3 TNS (core tension / what is at stake) 4 GL (goal) 5 ACT or NXT (action boundary / next) 6 SEC / ASM (secured vs assumed) 7 AST / GAP / REC (assets, gaps, recovery) 8 EXP / EXC / RBX (export honesty, rebase) 9 CAPS / VIS / CONF / SFL (completeness self-declaration)

ENRICHMENT (profile-specific, after the surface): 10 RSK (C1+) 11 DEC / LCK / CSTR (C2+) 12 TOPO / DEP / ARF / SRC (C3+) 13 HIS / PREF / RAT (C4+) 14 VAL / SIG / NTH / EVID (C5)

SENTINEL: 15 CTL / END


- **Never silently drop** lines 1–5 and 8 (identity, state, tension, goal, action,
  export/rebase).
- Lines 6–7 and 9 are omitted **only** when there is genuinely nothing to report
  (no gaps, no assumed items). If space forces omitting a *required* asset or a
  load-bearing SEC/ASM, it goes into `GAP`/`REC` and `RBX` is floored (see 2.7).

### 2.3 No Silent Escalation
A Provisional / Assumed item may never be treated as Secured without new recorded
evidence. `ASM` items stay `ASM` until an `EVID` or test line promotes them.

### 2.4 Active Asset Zero-Loss
A required active asset may not be silently dropped. If it is not fully present, it
must appear in `AST` (marked required) **and** in `GAP`/`REC` with a recovery path.

### 2.5 Completeness Cross-Check
If a tape claims `SFL=yes` or `CAPS=full` (or an equivalent readable statement), then
**every asset marked `required` must be fully present** in the tape. "Fully present"
means the actual content needed for continuation is inlined, not merely named or
summarized. If any required asset is summary-only, external, or missing:

- set `SFL=partial` (or `no`),
- set `CAPS=partial` (or lower),
- list it in `GAP`,
- provide recovery in `REC`.

Mitigations, actions, or constraints that *depend on* a required asset are valid only
if that asset is fully present or routed through `GAP`/`REC`. Violation is a protocol
integrity (honesty) failure, not a style issue.

### 2.6 No Fake Export
Never claim file creation, download, upload, or full preservation that did not occur.
`EXP` records the *actual* method; `EXC` records confidence in that claim.

### 2.7 Truncation Sentinel  (geometry, not a remembered rule)
Every **complete** tape ends with:

CTL=<pass|needs_review|fail> END=<id>


Loader rule:

> If the tape does **not** end with `END=<id>`, it was **truncated.** Treat every item
> after the last complete line as *possibly missing* — never infer "none." Floor
> `RBX` to at least `recommended`; if a required-asset line, a `SEC`/`ASM` line, or
> the `TNS` line falls in the cut zone, floor `RBX` to `required`. A missing `END=`
> means "cut," not "complete and empty."

Because `END=` is the last line *by construction*, a cut tape is automatically one that
lacks it. No model has to "remember to declare a cut" — the shape tells the loader.

### 2.8 Rebase
Rebase consolidates deltas into a cleaner Master. **See 08 for the full rules.**

## 08_REBASE  (full rules)

Rebase consolidates deltas and accumulated material into a cleaner **Master** that can
stand alone.

**Preserve**
- project identity and current phase
- active meaning that still has force (both pillars)
- the Decision Surface (action boundary, lock-ins, Secured/Assumed, core tension)
- significant assets (with required status)
- known gaps and recovery paths
- export honesty and confidence
- high-force causal / dependency notes

**Remove or demote**
- repeated boilerplate
- inactive history
- redundant evidence
- retired or superseded material
- low-signal prose
- absorbed delta detail

**Guiding rule:** *Meaning survives. Weight does not.*

- All Zero-Loss (2.4), Completeness Cross-Check (2.5), and No Silent Escalation (2.3)
  rules apply **during rebase**.
- **Delta / incremental tapes** are the preferred lightweight pattern *between* rebases.
- **Rebase when** a chain becomes long, noisy, or hard to traverse — produce a fresh
  Master, set the new tape's `PAR`/`RTT` correctly, and let the old chain be history.
