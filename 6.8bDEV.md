## 00_PURPOSE

6.8bDEV tests three properties at once:

1. **Semantic continuity posture** — P-C-T-R considers continuity before profile selection:
   Preserve what must not disappear; Carry what must survive the boundary; Transfer it so it
   arrives usable; then Record the established payload faithfully.
2. **Recoverable force** — a clear decision surface, agency, and epistemic hygiene, so a
   successor can actually *continue* the work, not just read what happened.
3. **Truncation resistance** — prefix-valid structure and ordered degradation, so the tape
   stays useful even when the output window is hard-cut.

Core principle:

> You are not minimizing tokens. You are **maximizing recoverable continuity per available
> token.** The profile budget is the transfer constraint, not the consideration horizon.
> The protected decision surface is the truncation-survival core. Useful restoration is the
> objective. Compression is a tool, not the goal. Smallness is not a virtue.

Design note (for maintainers, not loaders):

> The floor is built as **semantic posture + transport geometry**, not as an expanding list
> of corrective rules. P-C-T-R establishes the continuity field first. The selected C-profile
> then renders that field through the existing front cluster, enrichment tiers, and sentinel.
> The template is transport geometry, not a checklist defining completeness.

## 00A_P-C-T-R SEMANTIC POSTURE  (development candidate)

P-C-T-R is the common semantic posture for **all C0–C5 profiles** in 6.8bDEV:

- **Preserver** — recognise consequential available reality that should not disappear through
  premature reduction. Preserve rationale, scars, unresolved reasoning, dependencies,
  relationships, assets, and other continuation-relevant meaning when they matter.
- **Carrier** — keep the causal, relational, and intellectual structure that must survive the
  discontinuity. The crossing, not the producer's neatness, is the concern.
- **Transfer** — prefer usable arrival. The receiving context should be able to continue,
  reconstruct judgement, and act without avoidable rediscovery.
- **Recorder** — after continuity scope is understood, represent the selected payload faithfully
  and coherently in the chosen v6.8 profile and mode.

**Order is intentional:** continuity first; recording second.

P-C-T-R determines **what deserves consideration**. The C-profile determines **what can be
transferred in this horizon/budget**. A lower C-profile creates stronger selection pressure over
the same broadly considered continuity field; it does not instruct the producer to think about
less reality first.

P-C-T-R is an experimental semantic integration, not a claimed universal AI primitive. Its value
must be judged from artifacts and cold recovery, not from the name, producer self-report, or
protocol status.

## 01_DESIGN_POSTURE

- Keep the floor **low** — meaning low *capability* to load (a 14B can read it).
  "Low floor" does **not** mean "few tokens." A 500-token tape is trivially loadable.
- **Prefix-valid is mandatory.** The beginning of every tape is already a usable
  continuity artifact.
- **Recoverable continuity per available token is the primary success metric.**
- **Decision-surface per surviving token is the protected truncation metric.**
- **Agency and epistemic hygiene are protected** (Secured vs Assumed, no silent
  escalation, no simulation laundering).
- **Two first-class modes:** Readable and Compact.
- **Graceful degradation:** protect the decision surface as long as possible; cut
  from the tail, never from the middle of the surface.
- **Optional richness never raises the minimum landing requirement.**
- **Consider broadly; transfer selectively.** C0-C5 vary transfer horizon, not the semantic
  consideration horizon.

## 02_CORE_RULES

### 2.1 Prefix-Valid Rule
The beginning of every tape forms a usable minimum continuity artifact. Later material
only enriches. A cut from the tail must never remove the front cluster before it
removes the enrichment and the sentinel.

### 2.2 Ordered Degradation — the Front Cluster
The **decision surface is one front block.** Fill top-to-bottom; enrichment and the
sentinel come only *after* the surface.

FRONT CLUSTER (the decision surface — never split, never pushed to the tail): 1 IDN PAR RTT VER PFL (identity; MOD optional) 2 STT (state / current force) 3 TNS (core tension / what is at stake) 4 GL (goal) 5 ACT or NXT (action boundary / next) 6 SEC / ASM (secured vs assumed) 7 AST / GAP / REC (assets, gaps, recovery) 8 EXP / EXC / RBX (export honesty, rebase) 9 CAPS / VIS / CONF / SFL (completeness self-declaration)

ENRICHMENT (profile-specific, after the surface): 10 RSK (C1+) 11 DEC / LCK / CSTR (C2+) 12 TOPO / DEP / ARF / SRC (C3+) 13 HIS / PREF / RAT (C4+) 14 VAL / SIG / NTH / EVID (C5)

SENTINEL: 15 CTL / END


- **Never silently drop** lines 1–5 and 8 (identity, state, tension, goal, action,
  export/rebase).
- Lines 6–7 and 9 are omitted **only** when there is genuinely nothing to report
  (no gaps, no assumed items). If space forces omitting a *required* asset or a
  load-bearing SEC/ASM, it goes into `GAP`/`REC` and `RBX` is floored (see 2.7).

#### 2.2A Semantic Promotion

Enrichment tiers specify where extra detail normally lives; they do **not** forbid load-bearing
meaning from appearing earlier. If P-C-T-R determines that a historical scar, rationale,
dependency, preference, unresolved idea, or other continuity is necessary to understand `STT`,
`TNS`, `GL`, `ACT`, `SEC/ASM`, `AST/GAP/REC`, or another protected field, preserve that meaning
there. Do not postpone essential continuity merely because its dedicated enrichment atom appears
at a later tier.

### 2.3 No Silent Escalation
A Provisional / Assumed item may never be treated as Secured without new recorded
evidence. `ASM` items stay `ASM` until an `EVID` or test line promotes them.

### 2.4 Active Asset Zero-Loss
A required active asset may not be silently dropped. If it is not fully present, it
must appear in `AST` (marked required) **and** in `GAP`/`REC` with a recovery path.

### 2.5 Completeness Cross-Check
If a tape claims `SFL=yes` or `CAPS=full` (or an equivalent readable statement), then
**every asset marked `required` must be fully present** in the tape. "Fully present"
means the actual content needed for continuation is inlined, not merely named or
summarized. If any required asset is summary-only, external, or missing:

- set `SFL=partial` (or `no`),
- set `CAPS=partial` (or lower),
- list it in `GAP`,
- provide recovery in `REC`.

Mitigations, actions, or constraints that *depend on* a required asset are valid only
if that asset is fully present or routed through `GAP`/`REC`. Violation is a protocol
integrity (honesty) failure, not a style issue.

### 2.6 No Fake Export
Never claim file creation, download, upload, or full preservation that did not occur.
`EXP` records the *actual* method; `EXC` records confidence in that claim.

### 2.7 Truncation Sentinel  (geometry, not a remembered rule)
Every **complete** tape ends with:

CTL=<pass|needs_review|fail> END=<id>


Loader rule:

> If the tape does **not** end with `END=<id>`, it was **truncated.** Treat every item
> after the last complete line as *possibly missing* — never infer "none." Floor
> `RBX` to at least `recommended`; if a required-asset line, a `SEC`/`ASM` line, or
> the `TNS` line falls in the cut zone, floor `RBX` to `required`. A missing `END=`
> means "cut," not "complete and empty."

Because `END=` is the last line *by construction*, a cut tape is automatically one that
lacks it. No model has to "remember to declare a cut" — the shape tells the loader.

### 2.8 Rebase
Rebase consolidates deltas into a cleaner Master. **See 08 for the full rules.**

## 03_TWO FIRST-CLASS MODES

- **Readable Mode** — clear Markdown sections and short prose. Preferred for mixed
  human/AI use.
- **Compact Mode** — short atoms and tuples. Preferred under tight budgets or pure
  AI-to-AI transfer.
- **Hybrid** — readable headers + compact body. Allowed and encouraged when useful.

Readable equivalents use the **same semantics** with English labels:
Action Boundary, Lock-in, Secured / Assumed, Rebase, Gap, Recovery, Dependency,
Single-file status. A mode is a *rendering*, not a different meaning.

## 04_PROTECTED DECISION SURFACE  (authoritative)

This is the **single** authoritative definition of what must be protected. It is the
front cluster in 2.2.

**Minimum viable Decision Surface** (= the C0 front cluster, all 9 lines):

1. Current state / force — `STT`
2. Core tension / what is at stake — `TNS`
3. Goal — `GL`
4. Action Boundary (preferred) — `ACT` = Do Now / Investigate / Defer / Do Not Yet;
   or a clear `NXT` at extreme compression
5. Secured vs Assumed — `SEC` / `ASM`
6. Critical gaps + required assets (with `REC` if incomplete) — `AST` / `GAP` / `REC`
7. Export honesty + confidence — `EXP` / `EXC`
8. Rebase status — `RBX`
9. Completeness — `CAPS` / `VIS` / `CONF` / `SFL`

**Enriched Decision Surface** (add when the tier allows): risks, decisions, lock-ins,
constraints, topology, dependencies, artifacts, sources, history, preferences,
rationale, validation, signoff, northstar, evidence (see 07). These tier boundaries govern
normal rendering depth, not semantic eligibility; 2.2A permits load-bearing meaning to promote
forward when continuity requires it.

**Soft Depth** (what a *loader* must be able to answer from available content):
authority posture (from the header) · identity honesty · what still has force ·
critical gaps · one safe next action. Declare the handoff **under-dense** if almost
nothing remains under either pillar.

## 05_DUAL PILLAR

- **Pillar 1 — Cognitive Orientation:** the high-signal stance, the core tension, and
  the key rationales. *What is true, what is at stake, why.*
- **Pillar 2 — Asset Registry:** significant assets only, each with status,
  required-for-continuation, and freshness when useful. *What we need to keep going.*

The Protected Decision Surface (04) is the load-bearing intersection of the two: the
stance plus the assets plus the action.

## 07_PROFILE SYSTEM

All profiles use **P-C-T-R**. Profiles are transfer-horizon / channel-budget choices, not weaker
or stronger continuity thinking. P-C-T-R considers the broad continuity field first; the selected
profile then applies progressively stronger or weaker transfer selection pressure.

**Full C0–C5 option suite:**

| Profile | Friendly use | Nominal budget | Semantic posture |
|---|---|---:|---|
| C0 | Checkpoint / Safe Action | ~500 | P-C-T-R |
| C1 | Lean continuation | ~1000 | P-C-T-R |
| C2 | Working / Standard high-force | ~1500 | P-C-T-R |
| C3 | Deep / architectural | ~2000 | P-C-T-R |
| C4 | Macro / milestone | ~3000 | P-C-T-R |
| C5 | Full-intent / broadest defined profile | ~4000 | P-C-T-R |

`C5` is the broadest defined v6.8bDEV profile; it is **not** a claim that every worthwhile
continuity item physically fit. `END=` proves that a volume ended cleanly, not that inaccessible
or non-carried reality never existed. Completeness declarations remain governed by 2.5.


| Prof | Budget | Name              | Adds (each a new *kind*, never padding)                |
|:----:|-------:|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------------|
| `C0` | ~500   | Checkpoint / Safe Action| the **full decision surface** (front cluster, all 9)    |
| `C1` | ~1000  | Lean continuation  | + `RSK`                                                |
| `C2` | ~1500  | Working / Standard high-force| + `DEC` `LCK` `CSTR`                                    |
| `C3` | ~2000  | Deep / architectural| + `TOPO` `DEP` `ARF` `SRC`                             |
| `C4` | ~3000  | Macro / milestone  | + `HIS` `PREF` `RAT`                                    |
| `C5` | ~4000  | Full-intent / broadest profile     | + `VAL` `SIG` `NTH` `EVID`                             |

- **Cumulative / monotonic:** a `Cn` is a full `C(n-1)` with its tier added. Cutting a
  `C5` to 2000 tokens yields a valid `C3`; to 500 yields a valid `C0`. That
  monotonicity *is* the truncation guarantee.
- **Selection triggers:** unsure / safe minimum → `C0` · quick real handoff → `C1` ·
  normal rich handoff → `C2` · multi-file / dependency / debugging → `C3` ·
  milestone / cross-session → `C4` · audit / formal / rebase candidate → `C5`.
- **Anti-aspirational:** profile labels must match the substance delivered. A `C3`
  that only carries a `C1` surface is an honesty failure, not a typo.
- **Early-action guidance:** under elevated truncation risk, keep `ACT`/`NXT` early
  (it already is, at line 5). This is guidance, not a hard requirement.

## 08_REBASE  (full rules)

Rebase consolidates deltas and accumulated material into a cleaner **Master** that can
stand alone.

**Preserve**
- project identity and current phase
- active meaning that still has force (both pillars)
- the Decision Surface (action boundary, lock-ins, Secured/Assumed, core tension)
- significant assets (with required status)
- known gaps and recovery paths
- export honesty and confidence
- high-force causal / dependency notes

**Remove or demote**
- repeated boilerplate
- inactive history
- redundant evidence
- retired or superseded material
- low-signal prose
- absorbed delta detail

**Guiding rule:** *Meaning survives. Weight does not.*

- All Zero-Loss (2.4), Completeness Cross-Check (2.5), and No Silent Escalation (2.3)
  rules apply **during rebase**.
- **Delta / incremental tapes** are the preferred lightweight pattern *between* rebases.
- **Rebase when** a chain becomes long, noisy, or hard to traverse — produce a fresh
  Master, set the new tape's `PAR`/`RTT` correctly, and let the old chain be history.

## 09_SUCCESS TEST

A 6.8bDEV tape succeeds when a competent successor can:

1. Complete Soft Depth from the available content, **even if truncated.**
2. Recover the main stance and the critical tension (`STT` + `TNS`).
3. See a usable Action Boundary (or clear `NXT`).
4. Distinguish Secured from Assumed where it matters.
5. Identify critical gaps and required assets (and `REC` if incomplete).
6. Take a safe next action.
7. Trust the export and preservation claims.
8. Detect truncation: a tape not ending in `END=<id>` is treated as cut, with
   `RBX` floored accordingly.

> **Surviving truncation while losing the decision surface is a failure of the hybrid
> goal.** A tape that keeps the action but drops the tension, the Secured/Assumed
> line, or a required asset has failed even if it "survived."

## 10_TEMPLATES  (the floor, fill-in-the-blank)

P-C-T-R establishes the continuity field first. The model then fills the selected profile from
that field, top-to-bottom. The template is **transport geometry, not the definition of
completeness.** Do not stop merely because every visible slot has a plausible value while
consequential profile-eligible continuity remains uncarried. If the physical output is cut before
the tail, the missing `END=` *is* the truncation declaration.

Common semantic prelude for every profile: `TOOL=P-C-T-R` — Preserve → Carry → Transfer → Record.

## 11_WHAT 6.8 EXPLICITLY REFUSES

- Making compact atoms mandatory for basic landing.
- Treating minimal token count as success.
- Raising the capability floor against constrained models.
- Silent loss of required assets or lock-in awareness.
- Claiming perfect 100% transfer.
- Aspirational profile labeling.
- Non-self-contained floor specifications.
- Treating a truncated tape (missing `END=`) as complete.
- Treating a lower C-profile as permission to consider only shallow project reality.
- Treating a filled template as complete when visible, consequential, profile-eligible continuity remains uncarried.
- Treating P-C-T-R as proven merely because it is the development candidate semantic posture.
