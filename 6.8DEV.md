00_PURPOSE6.8 delivers two properties at once:High recoverable force — clear decision surface, agency, and epistemic hygiene so a successor can actually continue the work.
Truncation resistance — prefix-valid structure and ordered degradation so the tape remains useful even when cut.

Core principle
You are not minimizing tokens.
You are maximizing preserved decision-surface per available token.
The budget is the constraint; the decision surface is the objective.Useful restoration remains the primary goal.
Compression is a tool, not the goal.
Smallness is not a virtue.01_DESIGN_POSTUREKeep the floor low and honest.
Prefix-valid is mandatory.
Force density is the primary success metric.
Agency and epistemic hygiene are protected.
Two first-class modes: Readable and Compact.
Graceful degradation: protect the decision surface as long as possible.
Optional richness must never raise the minimum landing requirement.
Meaning survives.

Violation is a protocol integrity (honesty) failure.
